Delete your Akhu account

For the Akhu wallpaper and theme app by Akhu Studios.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Akhu and make sure you're signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the Settings gear at the top of the screen, then Delete account (under Account).
  3. Confirm with your password, or by choosing your Google account if you signed in with Google.

Your account and data are deleted straight away, and you're signed out.

Without the app

Email akhuwallpapers@gmail.com from the email address you use for Akhu, with the subject "Delete my Akhu account". If you signed in with Google, send it from that Google account's address. We'll confirm, and delete the account within 30 days.

What's deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password, or the link to your Google account).
  • Your profile: name, username, pronouns, bio and profile picture.
  • Every wallpaper and theme you posted, with its pictures, videos and other files, and the full version of any post you were selling.
  • Comments you wrote, and comments on your posts.
  • Your likes, who you follow and who follows you.
  • Your activity notices, the accounts you blocked and the posts you hid.

Copies in our hosting provider's backups (Google Firebase) may last up to 30 more days before they're overwritten.

What's kept, and for how long

  • Purchase and sales records (what was bought or sold, when, the price and each side's share, and payout records) are kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require, usually up to 7 years. They aren't used for anything else.
  • Reports you made about other accounts or content are kept while we review them.
  • Posts you bought from others stop being available to you once your account is deleted. Google Play keeps its own record of your payments under Google's privacy policy.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • A post: open it in Akhu and tap Delete. Its files, likes and comments are removed.
  • A comment you wrote, or one on your post: long-press it and tap Delete.
  • Your profile details: edit or clear them in Profile → Edit profile.
  • An activity notice: swipe it away in the Inbox.
  • Anything else: email akhuwallpapers@gmail.com saying what you'd like deleted, and we'll do it within 30 days.

See also the Akhu Privacy Policy.
