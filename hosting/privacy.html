Akhu Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Akhu is a wallpaper and theme app for Android made by Akhu Studios ("we"). This policy explains what Akhu collects, why, who can see it and how to remove it. It covers the Akhu app and the online services it uses. Questions: akhuwallpapers@gmail.com.

In short: most of what you make stays on your phone. We don't sell your data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Akhu Premium removes them. Payments go through Google Play, so we never see your card details.

Akhu needs a free account, for both the free version and Akhu Premium. You sign in with an email address and password, or with your Google account.

Akhu asks for these phone permissions, each only for the feature named:

  • Photos you pick: through Android's own pickers (no storage permission), to make wallpapers. Akhu sees only the pictures you choose.
  • Notifications: to tell you about likes, comments and shares.
  • Approximate location: for the weather widget.
  • Calendar: for the events widget. Your calendar is read on the phone and never uploaded.
  • Notification access (optional): for the music widget, to show what's playing. Akhu reads media controls only, never the text of your notifications.

We don't collect your contacts or precise location, and we don't collect analytics about how you use the app.

How we use it

  • Sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • Show your posts, profile, likes and comments to the people allowed to see them.
  • Let you follow people and see their posts.
  • Notify you about activity on your posts.
  • Sell posts for you and unlock posts you buy: we check each purchase with Google Play, record the sale, and work out the seller's earnings.
  • Give you Akhu Premium when you buy it.
  • Review reports and remove content or accounts that break Akhu's rules, and keep blocked accounts away from you.
  • Show ads in the free version.
  • Show the weather, your next calendar event or what's playing in the widgets you add.

Content checks run on your phone. Before a picture or video is posted publicly, or used as your profile picture, Akhu checks it for explicit content with a model built into the app. Your pictures aren't sent anywhere for this. If one fails, it isn't posted.

We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Ads

The free version of Akhu shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, information about your device and the app, your IP address (from which it estimates your rough location) and the ads you see and tap. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Akhu asks for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Akhu Premium shows no ads, and AdMob isn't started for Premium members.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Buying and selling

Buying. Akhu Premium and posts for sale are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. Google tells us that the purchase went through, with its order number and a purchase token, and we keep a record of what you bought so it stays unlocked for you. A seller can see that their post sold, but not who bought it.

Selling. When you put a post up for sale, everyone sees a watermarked preview; only you and the people who buy it get the full version. We keep a record of each sale of your post (the date, the price and your share) and your earnings totals, which only you can see.

Payouts. Sellers will be paid through Stripe. To receive payouts you'll set up an account with Stripe, which collects the identity, bank and tax details it needs (for example, for US tax forms) under Stripe's privacy policy. Akhu doesn't store your bank details or tax ID; we keep only your Stripe account's ID and a record of each payout. You must be 18 or older to receive payouts.

Who can see your information

  • Everyone using Akhu can see your public posts (or their preview, if they're for sale), your profile (display name, username, pronouns, bio, profile picture), who you follow, who follows you, your comments, and that you liked a public post.
  • Only you can see your private posts, your email address, your purchases, your sales and earnings, and the activity notices about your posts.
  • Posts can be shared outside Akhu. Anyone with a public post's link can open its picture (the preview, for a post for sale).

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the services that run Akhu:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) stores your account, profile, posts, purchases and sales, and runs the purchase checks. Google processes this data for us under the Firebase terms and Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play handles payments and tells us about purchases and refunds.
  • Google AdMob shows ads in the free version, as described under Ads.
  • Stripe pays sellers, as described under Payouts.
  • Open-Meteo receives your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) when the weather widget fetches a forecast. See Open-Meteo's terms.

Like any online service, these receive your device's IP address when the app connects. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm.

Storage, security and deletion

Your data is stored on Google's servers through Firebase and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let only you change or delete your posts, profile and notices, and only our servers can record purchases and sales.

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete things yourself at any time:

  • A post: open it and tap Delete. The picture or video, its likes and comments, and the full version of a post for sale are removed. People who bought it lose access to it in Akhu.
  • A comment you wrote, or one on your post: long-press it.
  • An activity notice: swipe it away in the Inbox.
  • Your account: Settings → Delete account. Your account, profile, posts, comments and notices are deleted. See how to delete your account, including without the app.
  • Things on your phone: delete them in the app, or uninstall Akhu.

You can also ask us to delete your account at akhuwallpapers@gmail.com, from the address you signed up with; we do it within 30 days. Copies in Firebase backups may last up to a further 30 days. Records of purchases, sales and payouts are kept after an account is deleted for as long as tax and accounting laws require (usually up to 7 years), and are used for nothing else.

Your choices

  • Post privately instead of publicly.
  • Block accounts or hide posts you don't want to see, and report anything that breaks Akhu's rules. The person you block or report isn't told. Manage blocked accounts in Settings.
  • Get Akhu Premium for no ads, or limit ad personalisation as described under Ads.
  • Turn off Akhu's notifications, location, calendar or notification access any time in Android Settings → Apps → Akhu.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, at akhuwallpapers@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have more rights under local law, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Akhu isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it. Receiving payouts for sales requires being 18 or older.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
